Advanced comprehension techniques

Read for a central idea

After reading, explain the main message in your own words. A strong summary includes the important point without unnecessary detail.

Support an inference

An inference is a reasonable conclusion based on clues. Locate the sentences that make your answer likely, not merely possible.

Interpret language

Study how metaphors, tone, and word choice shape meaning. Decide what a phrase means in its sentence and the passage as a whole.

Evaluate evidence

Ask whether an observation truly supports a conclusion. Distinguish measurements, opinions, possibilities and proven claims.

Consider the author

Identify purpose: to inform, persuade, entertain, reflect or compare ideas. Notice how examples and structure help that purpose.

Check every choice

A tempting answer may sound sensible but go beyond what the passage supports. Choose the answer with the strongest textual evidence.
