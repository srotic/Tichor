Messages

Internet messaging: recipients do not need to have Tichor open. Messages are saved to Supabase and appear when they reconnect. Push alerts require notification permission and working server setup. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted in this version.

Background notifications

Receive new-message and incoming-call alerts even when this app is closed. Enable separately on each device. On iPhone, add Tichor to your Home Screen first.

Internet voice & video calls

No contact selected

Tichor can alert an offline recipient with Web Push when notifications are enabled. Tapping the alert opens the incoming call in Tichor; answering requires the app to be open. Browser microphone/camera permission is required. Calls use WebRTC; some networks cannot connect without a relay server.

Ringtones play only while Tichor is open. When closed, your phone controls the push-notification sound.

No active call

If you cannot hear the other person, tap Enable call sound and check your device volume.

No passphrase is needed for new messages. To read old messages encrypted in v8 or earlier, enter the original shared passphrase. Never enter your account password here.
