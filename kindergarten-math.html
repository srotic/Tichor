For grown-ups and teachers

Keep sessions short

Eight problems per session typically work best as a brief activity. Pause when your child loses interest.

Use real objects

Let children count buttons, blocks, fruits or toys. Encourage pointing to each object once.

Ask “How do you know?”

Children learn more from explaining their thinking than from guessing quickly. Discuss mistakes kindly.

Build number sense

Practice recognizing numerals and relating each numeral to a group of objects before focusing on speed.

Connect math to play

Look for shapes, repeating patterns and comparisons while cooking, building or walking outside.

Check learning gently

Progress is recorded locally only. Scores reflect practice, not a diagnosis or formal assessment.
