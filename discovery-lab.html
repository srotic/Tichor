DNA double helix

Explore the code of life

DNA has two complementary strands. Adenine (A) pairs with thymine (T), while cytosine (C) pairs with guanine (G).

Change the helix turns to see how the visualized strands wind around one another. This controls the illustration, not the DNA sequence.

Orbital motion

Why do planets orbit?

Gravity pulls a planet toward its star while the planet's motion carries it forward, producing an orbit. Real planetary orbits are generally elliptical.

The speed slider changes only the animation speed. It does not calculate orbital mechanics or represent actual planetary periods.

The beating heart

Meet your cardiovascular system

The heart pumps blood through pulmonary and systemic circuits. Electrical signals coordinate the contraction of its chambers.

The rate slider adjusts a symbolic heartbeat animation; it is not an ECG, diagnostic tool, or anatomically accurate cardiac model.
