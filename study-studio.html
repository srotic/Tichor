Lecture Notes Study Studio

Turn your own notes into source-linked summaries, flashcards, and practice questions. Choose local extractive study tools or optional on-device AI comprehension when your browser supports it.

Privacy: Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a Tichor server by this page. PDF, DOCX and PPTX extraction libraries download from third-party CDNs. Scanned PDFs without selectable text require OCR, which is not included. The extractive tools are not generative AI; optional on-device AI is available only in supported browsers.

1. Add your lecture materials

Up to 10 files, 15 MB each. PowerPoint and Word must use modern .pptx/.docx formats.

Ready to process notes.

2. Generate study materials

Items are generated only from sentences extracted from your materials. If there is insufficient suitable content, fewer items will be produced. Difficulty controls and true semantic understanding require a compatible language model.

✦ AI-powered lecture comprehension

Generate conceptual summaries, explanation-based MCQs, flashcards, or application questions grounded in the notes you processed above. This feature uses a browser-provided on-device language model when available; no paid cloud API is connected.

Checking browser AI support…

Browser support is limited, especially on iOS. Initial model download may be large. The AI may make mistakes: review all claims and references against your notes. If unavailable, use the existing free source-extraction tools above. Your notes are not sent to a Tichor server by this AI feature.
