Lecture Notes Study Studio

Turn your own notes into source-linked summaries, flashcards, and practice questions. This first release uses local, extractive processing rather than a paid AI service, so results depend on the wording and structure of your notes.

Privacy: Files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a Tichor server by this page. PDF, DOCX and PPTX extraction libraries download from third-party CDNs. Scanned PDFs without selectable text require OCR, which is not included. This is not generative AI and cannot reliably create sophisticated case studies or unlimited unique questions.

1. Add your lecture materials

Up to 10 files, 15 MB each. PowerPoint and Word must use modern .pptx/.docx formats.

Ready to process notes.

2. Generate study materials

Items are generated only from sentences extracted from your materials. If there is insufficient suitable content, fewer items will be produced. Difficulty controls and true semantic understanding require a compatible language model.
