How to approach CARS

Foundations of Comprehension

Identify the central thesis, authorial attitude, paragraph function and the meaning of claims in context. Avoid choices that are too broad or too absolute.

Reasoning Within the Text

Trace how evidence supports conclusions, expose unstated assumptions, compare viewpoints and identify a counterexample that undermines an argument.

Reasoning Beyond the Text

Apply the passage's logic to a new hypothetical, anticipate what the author would favor and select what would strengthen or weaken an inference.

A practical pacing benchmark is approximately 10 minutes per passage, including its questions. Your reading process should identify structure rather than attempt to memorize every detail. For accurate format details, consult AAMC's official CARS overview.
