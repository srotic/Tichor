Explore science visually

Rotate a three-dimensional molecular model, trace cardiac circulation, and experiment with enzyme kinetics. These are educational illustrations and simplified models—not clinical or laboratory instruments.

Cardiovascular anatomy: follow the blood

Choose a step to trace the simplified path through the four chambers and major vessels. Blue indicates relatively deoxygenated blood; red indicates relatively oxygenated blood.

Schematic, not to scale. The pathways shown between chambers are conceptual; the full route includes pulmonary and systemic circulation.

3D molecular viewer: methane (CH₄)

Drag to rotate the ball-and-stick model. Carbon is dark gray; hydrogen atoms are pale. The tetrahedral geometry has approximately 109.5° H–C–H bond angles.

Rendered with perspective projection in the browser; no external 3D library or server required.

MCAT experiment: enzyme kinetics

Explore a simplified Michaelis–Menten model, v = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S]). Change substrate concentration, Vmax and Km to observe the initial reaction rate.

Idealized single-substrate steady-state model. It omits cooperativity, inhibitors, enzyme depletion and experimental uncertainty.
