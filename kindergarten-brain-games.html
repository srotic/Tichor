Tips for parents and educators

Play together

Ask children how they noticed a pattern or found a match. Their explanations matter more than their score.

Short and joyful

Try one five-round game at a time, with breaks and plenty of encouragement.

Build real-life connections

Sort toys by shape, make pairs of socks, copy movement patterns, and look for differences outdoors.

Respect different learning needs

Offer help reading questions, turn off audio when preferred, and repeat games without pressure.
