Strategies for stronger thinking

Look for a rule

In sequences, check how numbers change: adding, subtracting, doubling, or alternating steps.

Separate clues

For logic puzzles, list what must be true and eliminate options that contradict a clue.

Make a picture

Draw a grid for spatial problems or number puzzles. Visualizing a problem can simplify it.

Check all answers

Some choices look possible but do not fit every part of the question. Verify before selecting.

Remember in groups

For Memory Matrix, name the symbols in small groups to make recall easier.

Learn from mistakes

After each puzzle, explain why the right choice works. Reasoning matters more than speed.
